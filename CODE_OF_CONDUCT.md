# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in this project. All contributors, users, maintainers, and other participants are expected to communicate and behave professionally and respectfully.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives. Everyone should feel safe to participate, ask questions, report problems, and share ideas.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Using respectful and inclusive language.
- Being considerate and professional when communicating with others.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Respecting different opinions, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Helping others when reasonably possible.
- Giving constructive and useful feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately provoking other participants.
- Publishing another person's private or personal information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate comments.
- Threatening or abusive behavior.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions or activities.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate or unprofessional.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards described in this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may take appropriate action in response to behavior that violates these standards. They may remove inappropriate content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or take other reasonable measures when necessary to maintain a respectful project environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and activities, including the project's repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, comments, and other official project communication channels.

It may also apply to conduct outside these spaces when such conduct has a clear and significant impact on the safety or well-being of project participants or the project community.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, you are encouraged to report it to the project maintainers through the project's available communication channels.

Reports should include enough information for maintainers to understand what happened and take appropriate action. Reports will be handled as respectfully and confidentially as reasonably possible.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and take action based on the circumstances and available information.

## Enforcement Guidelines

When determining an appropriate response, maintainers may consider:

1. The nature and seriousness of the behavior.
2. Whether the behavior was intentional or repeated.
3. The effect of the behavior on other participants or the project community.
4. Whether the person involved acknowledges the issue and is willing to correct the behavior.
5. Any relevant circumstances surrounding the incident.

Possible responses may include:

- A private or public warning.
- Removal of inappropriate comments or contributions.
- Temporary restriction from participating in project activities.
- Permanent restriction from participating in the project.
- Reporting serious incidents to the appropriate authorities when required.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is based on commonly used open-source community guidelines and has been adapted for this project to establish clear expectations for respectful participation.

The project maintainers may update this Code of Conduct when necessary to improve clarity, safety, and inclusiveness.
